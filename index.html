How I Work

Design Creation Process

A simple, transparent 4-step workflow to turn your ideas into high-converting visual assets.

1

Brief & Idea

We discuss your brand goals, target audience, preferred colors, and key text offers via WhatsApp or Email.

2

Initial Drafts

I create 2–3 unique initial visual concepts in Canva Pro / Photoshop within 24–48 hours for your review.

3

Revisions & Polish

We refine colors, font hierarchy, alignment, and text formatting based on your exact feedback.

4

Final Delivery

You receive high-res 300DPI Print PDFs, digital PNGs/JPEGs, and editable Canva Pro templates.
